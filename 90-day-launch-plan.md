# Verzamar Technologies 90 Day Launch Plan

## Purpose

This plan turns the redesigned website into a practical visibility and enquiry system for Verzamar Technologies. It prioritizes Lagos and Nigeria first, while keeping the service language useful for clients in the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, Europe, and worldwide markets. It does not promise rankings, traffic, AI search inclusion, or a fixed number of leads.

## Days 1 to 30: Make the foundation trustworthy

The first month should make the website, business profile, contact paths, and measurement ready for real visitors.

Confirm the final business name, service areas, phone number, email, opening hours, consultation availability, package direction, privacy wording, terms, logo asset, and public domain. Once the domain is chosen, update the canonical origin, sitemap host, social preview details, and branded email paths.

Claim or verify the Google Business Profile if the business is eligible. Use the real business name, the most appropriate category, accurate service area details, current hours, phone, website, and genuine photographs. Complete the profile without creating duplicate listings or location pages that do not represent real service coverage.

Test the homepage, service pages, contact form, booking page, WhatsApp route, email links, newsletter form, article pages, sitemap, robots file, and custom 404 on real phones and desktop browsers. Record the current baseline for qualified enquiries, consultation bookings, calls, WhatsApp conversations, newsletter signups, Search Console queries, Business Profile interactions, and the pages that support those actions.

Publish the first three article promotions through owned channels. Use the small business website guide, the local SEO guide, and the conversion improvement guide as useful answers for consultants, coaches, and local service businesses. Link each article to a relevant service and to the Free Consultation page.

## Days 31 to 60: Build local relevance and useful demand

The second month should make the site easier to discover for real customer questions and easier to trust once someone arrives.

Create a simple local content map for Lagos, Abuja, Delta State, and any other service areas the business can genuinely support. Prioritize service intent over thin location pages. A useful page should explain the service, audience, area served, process, contact route, and a real reason the location matters.

Publish the next three articles: plumber and local service website design, why a business website is not generating enquiries, and signs a business needs a website redesign. Add internal links between articles, services, FAQs, the audit path, and the booking page. Share each article with one clear message rather than repeating a generic promotion.

Request genuine feedback from completed projects and ask permission before publishing a testimonial or project context. Add only verified details to the Work page. If a project result is not measured or approved, describe the project link without claiming a result.

Review the first month of search queries, Business Profile interactions, page engagement, calls, messages, form completion, and consultation bookings. Look for questions that visitors still ask. Turn those questions into FAQ improvements, service page edits, or future articles.

If the website receives relevant attention but few enquiries, improve the clearest friction point first. Test the opening message, primary call to action, form length, response expectation, proof, service area wording, or mobile spacing. Change one meaningful element at a time and record what changed.

## Days 61 to 90: Improve the system and prepare the next quarter

The third month should turn the early evidence into a repeatable content and lead process.

Create a monthly editorial rhythm with one substantial article, one service page improvement, one FAQ improvement, and one proof or portfolio update when there is genuine material to publish. Future topics can include website speed and performance, business website costs, website content and messaging, AI search visibility, website maintenance and security, and lead generation through websites.

Create a simple lead handling routine. Review new enquiries on a set schedule, respond using the preferred contact method, record the source and service interest, and note whether the enquiry was qualified. Do not report a lead as a sale unless the business has confirmed that outcome.

Connect Google Sheets, customer email notifications, newsletter automation, analytics, Search Console, Tag Manager, CRM, or a live chat provider only after the business authorizes each connection and the setup is tested. Keep the current website honest while those connections remain pending.

Review technical quality on mobile and desktop. Check performance, image weight, form errors, focus states, contrast, headings, metadata, internal links, structured data, sitemap access, and 404 behavior. Recheck dark mode after any component change. Keep reduced motion support enabled.

At day 90, produce a short review containing the articles published, service pages improved, qualified enquiries, booked consultations, contact sources, common questions, technical issues found, and the next three priorities. Use the review to decide whether the next quarter should focus on content, local visibility, conversion improvement, a package launch, or an authorized integration.

## Suggested measurement table

| Area | Useful observation | Avoid claiming |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local visibility | Search queries, Business Profile actions, calls, direction requests, and service area questions | A guaranteed local position |
| Website conversion | Qualified enquiries, consultation bookings, calls, messages, and completed forms | A universal conversion rate or guaranteed uplift |
| Content | Articles published, useful page visits, assisted enquiries, and customer questions answered | Search volume or traffic estimates that were not verified |
| Trust | Approved testimonials, live project links, response time, and customer feedback | Invented results, awards, or client credentials |
| Technical quality | Mobile checks, accessibility review, performance observations, sitemap and 404 status | A technical score as a business result |

## Decisions still needed

The plan needs the final public domain, logo, package and pricing direction, language list, legal review, analytics and Search Console authorization, email and newsletter provider choice, CRM decision, live chat provider choice, and approval for any future payment or automation connection.
